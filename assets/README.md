# Assets Folder

## Image Naming Convention

Place your before/after images here with the following naming:

### Format: `{location_id}_{intervention_type}.jpg`

### Location IDs:
- `Tun_Razak` - Jalan Tun Razak (Commercial)
- `Bangsar` - Bangsar South (Mixed Usage)
- `Cheras` - Cheras Utama (Residential)

### Intervention Types:
- `Baseline` - No intervention (before state)
- `Green` - Green Corridor (Trees)
- `Bike` - Bike Lane
- `Emergency` - Emergency Route
- `Flyover` - Highway Flyover
- `PublicTransport` - Public Transport

### Examples:
- `Tun_Razak_Baseline.jpg` - Before state at Tun Razak
- `Tun_Razak_Green.jpg` - After adding trees at Tun Razak
- `Bangsar_Bike.jpg` - After adding bike lane at Bangsar
- `Cheras_PublicTransport.jpg` - After adding public transport at Cheras

## Minimum Required Images (for prototype):

**Priority 1 (Must have):**
- `Tun_Razak_Baseline.jpg`

- `Bangsar_Baseline.jpg`

**Priority 2 (Nice to have):**

## How to Get Images:

1. **AI Generation** (Recommended):
   - Use ChatGPT DALL-E or Midjourney
   - Prompt: "A busy street in Kuala Lumpur with heavy traffic and pollution, urban setting"
   - Then: "Same street with green trees, bike lanes, and improved infrastructure"

2. **Stock Photos**:
   - Unsplash.com - Search "Kuala Lumpur", "urban planning"
   - Pexels.com - Search "city street", "traffic"

3. **Edit Existing Photos**:
   - Use Photoshop/GIMP to add trees, bike lanes, etc.

## Image Requirements:
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Size: 800x600 or 1200x800 (recommended)
- Quality: Clear, professional-looking
